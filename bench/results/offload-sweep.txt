Reading: a threshold that fires below the window's own budget converts one big read
into a pinned re-fetch the run then carries forever — which is why the low settings
cost more than turning the mechanism off. What this suite cannot yet show is the case
the mechanism exists for: a single result larger than the whole window, where an
inline read is not merely expensive but impossible. That needs field-scoped re-reads
(offset/limit paging that stays parseable), tracked as a follow-up.
